# ForeverLooter

ForeverLooter is derived from MoneyLooter 2.0.0 under the included MIT license.
The source changelog below documents the upstream release history.

## ForeverLooter fork

- Rebranded the addon, saved variables, slash commands, frames, and locale globals.
- Replaced the custom panel skin with Blizzard panel and control templates.
- Added ForeverTracker-style gold accents and inset styling to the loot view.
- Limited the addon manifest to WoW Forever interface 16001.
